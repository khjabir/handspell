A Handspell

Let your camera check your signing

Handspell watches your hand and tells you when you have the letter right. The video never leaves this device.

A

Teach Handspell your ISL alphabet. Sign each letter once while it watches. It takes about two minutes, and your hands are recognised far better afterwards.

Spell the word

    What did the hand spell?

    Reading fingerspelling is the harder half. Watch the hand, then type the word. No camera needed.

    Speed

    Letter rain

    Letters fall down your camera picture. Sign the lowest one before it lands. Each one you catch speeds things up.

    Spell anything

    Hold each letter until it appears. For a double letter, slide your hand a little to the side and hold it again. Lower your hand for a space.
